Use the controls to vary all four real parameters λ, μ, ν, ρ of Naruki’s family and watch the cubic surface and its 27 marked lines change. Moving λ, μ, ν changes the configuration within the family, while moving ρ toward zero follows its degeneration to the three coordinate planes x₀x₁x₂ = 0.
